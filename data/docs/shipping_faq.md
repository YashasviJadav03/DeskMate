# Shipping FAQ

## Shipping Options

| Method | Delivery Time | Cost |
|--------|--------------|------|
| Standard Shipping | 5–7 business days | Free on orders over $50 |
| Express Shipping | 2–3 business days | $9.99 |
| Next-Day Delivery | 1 business day | $19.99 |
| Same-Day Delivery | Same day (order by 12 PM) | $29.99 (select cities only) |

## Order Processing
All orders are processed within 1–2 business days. Orders placed after 5 PM EST or on weekends/holidays will be processed the next business day. You will receive a confirmation email with tracking information once your order ships.

## Tracking Your Order
1. Check your email for the shipping confirmation with a tracking number.
2. Visit **My Orders** in your ShopEase account to see real-time status.
3. Click the tracking number to view detailed delivery updates on the carrier's website.

## Shipping Restrictions
- We currently ship to all 50 US states, Puerto Rico, and select international destinations.
- Hazardous materials (batteries, flammable items) may have shipping restrictions.
- PO Boxes are supported only for Standard Shipping.
- Some oversized items may incur additional freight charges.

## Delivery Issues
- **Package not received**: If your tracking shows "delivered" but you haven't received it, wait 24 hours (carriers sometimes mark early). Then contact us.
- **Damaged package**: Take photos of the damage and contact us within 48 hours for a replacement.
- **Wrong item received**: Contact support immediately — we'll send the correct item and provide a return label for the wrong one.

## Holiday Shipping
During peak seasons (Black Friday, Christmas), shipping times may be extended by 1–3 days. Order early to ensure timely delivery.
