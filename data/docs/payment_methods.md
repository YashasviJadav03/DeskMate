# Payment Methods

## Accepted Payment Options
ShopEase accepts a wide range of payment methods for your convenience:

- **Credit/Debit Cards**: Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover
- **Digital Wallets**: Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayPal
- **Buy Now, Pay Later**: Afterpay (split into 4 interest-free payments), Klarna
- **ShopEase Gift Cards**: Can be combined with other payment methods
- **Bank Transfer**: Available for orders over $500

## Payment Security
All transactions are protected by 256-bit SSL encryption. We are PCI DSS compliant and never store your full credit card number on our servers. For added security, we support 3D Secure verification for credit card payments.

## Billing Issues

### Payment Declined
If your payment is declined, please check:
1. Card number, expiration date, and CVV are entered correctly.
2. Billing address matches what's on file with your bank.
3. Your card has sufficient funds or credit available.
4. Your bank hasn't blocked the transaction (call them to verify).

### Double Charged
If you see a duplicate charge, the second charge is usually a temporary authorization hold that will disappear within 3–5 business days. If it persists, contact us with your order number and we'll resolve it.

### Invoice Requests
Business customers can request an invoice by emailing billing@shopease.com with their order number and company details. Invoices are typically issued within 2 business days.

## Currency
All prices are listed in USD. International customers will be charged in USD and their bank will convert to local currency at the prevailing exchange rate. Currency conversion fees may apply.
