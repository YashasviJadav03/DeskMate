# Privacy Policy

## Information We Collect
ShopEase collects the following information to process your orders and improve your experience:

### Information You Provide
- Name, email address, phone number
- Shipping and billing addresses
- Payment information (processed securely, never stored in full)
- Account preferences and saved items
- Product reviews and feedback

### Information Collected Automatically
- Browser type and version
- IP address and general location
- Pages visited and time spent on our site
- Device type (desktop, mobile, tablet)
- Referral source (how you found us)

## How We Use Your Information
- **Order Processing**: To fulfill and ship your orders, process payments, and send order updates.
- **Customer Support**: To respond to your inquiries and resolve issues.
- **Personalization**: To recommend products based on your browsing and purchase history.
- **Marketing**: To send promotional emails (only with your consent, and you can unsubscribe anytime).
- **Analytics**: To improve our website, products, and services.

## Data Sharing
We do **not** sell your personal information to third parties. We share data only with:
- **Shipping carriers** (name and address for delivery)
- **Payment processors** (for secure transaction handling)
- **Analytics providers** (anonymized data only)
- **Law enforcement** (only when legally required)

## Your Rights
- **Access**: Request a copy of the data we hold about you.
- **Correction**: Update or correct inaccurate information.
- **Deletion**: Request deletion of your data (subject to legal retention requirements).
- **Opt-out**: Unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time via the link in the email footer.

## Data Retention
We retain your data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. After account deletion, data is removed within 30 days, except where required by law.

## Contact
For privacy-related inquiries, email privacy@shopease.com.
