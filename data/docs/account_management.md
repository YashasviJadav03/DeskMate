# Account Management

## Creating an Account
To create a ShopEase account:
1. Click **Sign Up** in the top right corner.
2. Enter your email address and choose a password (minimum 8 characters, must include a number and special character).
3. Verify your email by clicking the link we send you.
4. Complete your profile with your name and shipping address.

New accounts receive a **10% welcome discount** on their first order!

## Managing Your Account

### Update Personal Information
Go to **Account Settings > Profile** to update your name, email, phone number, or date of birth.

### Change Password
Go to **Account Settings > Security > Change Password**. You'll need to enter your current password and then your new one twice.

### Saved Addresses
You can save multiple shipping addresses under **Account Settings > Addresses**. Set a default address for faster checkout. You can store up to 10 addresses.

### Saved Payment Methods
Manage your saved cards and payment methods under **Account Settings > Payment Methods**. You can add, remove, or set a default payment method.

## Deleting Your Account
To delete your account, go to **Account Settings > Privacy > Delete Account**. This action is permanent and will:
- Remove all personal data within 30 days
- Cancel any active subscriptions
- Void any unused store credit (redeem first!)

You will receive a confirmation email. You have 14 days to reactivate before deletion is final.

## Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)
We strongly recommend enabling 2FA for added security. Go to **Account Settings > Security > Two-Factor Authentication** and follow the setup wizard. We support authenticator apps (Google Authenticator, Authy) and SMS verification.
