# Product Warranty

## Standard Warranty
All ShopEase products come with a **1-year limited warranty** that covers defects in materials and workmanship under normal use. This warranty is valid from the date of purchase.

## What's Covered
- Manufacturing defects (e.g., broken zippers, faulty seams, malfunctioning electronics)
- Materials that degrade abnormally under normal use
- Components that fail within the warranty period under normal operating conditions

## What's NOT Covered
- Damage from misuse, abuse, or neglect
- Normal wear and tear (fading, minor scratches, battery degradation)
- Damage caused by unauthorized modifications or repairs
- Water damage (unless the product is rated as waterproof)
- Cosmetic damage that doesn't affect functionality
- Damage caused by natural disasters or acts of God

## Extended Warranty
For select product categories (electronics, appliances, furniture), you can purchase an **Extended Protection Plan** at checkout:
- **2-Year Plan**: +15% of item price
- **3-Year Plan**: +25% of item price

Extended plans include accidental damage coverage and priority support.

## How to File a Warranty Claim
1. Go to **My Orders** and find the item.
2. Click **Warranty Claim** and describe the issue.
3. Upload photos showing the defect.
4. Our team will review within 3–5 business days.
5. If approved, you'll receive a replacement or store credit.

## Manufacturer Warranties
Some products also carry a separate manufacturer warranty. Check the product documentation for details. ShopEase warranty does not replace manufacturer warranties — they can be used together.
