# Contact Support

## Support Channels

### Live Chat
Available **24/7** through the chat widget on our website. Average response time: under 2 minutes. Best for quick questions and order issues.

### Email
Send us an email at **support@shopease.com**. We respond within 24 hours on business days. Best for detailed inquiries, complaints, and documentation-heavy requests.

### Phone
Call us at **1-800-SHOP-EZ** (1-800-746-7399).
- Monday to Friday: 8 AM – 10 PM EST
- Saturday: 9 AM – 6 PM EST
- Sunday: 10 AM – 4 PM EST

### Social Media
Reach out on Twitter (@ShopEaseHelp) or Facebook Messenger. We monitor social channels during business hours and typically respond within 1–3 hours.

## Before Contacting Support
To help us resolve your issue faster, please have the following ready:
- Your **order number** (found in your confirmation email or account)
- The **email address** associated with your account
- A **description** of the issue
- **Photos** of the problem (if applicable — e.g., damaged item)

## Complaint Resolution
We take all complaints seriously. Our resolution process:
1. **Acknowledgment**: You'll receive confirmation within 2 hours.
2. **Investigation**: Our team reviews within 1–3 business days.
3. **Resolution**: We'll offer a solution (refund, replacement, credit, or explanation).
4. **Follow-up**: We check in to make sure you're satisfied.

If you're not happy with the resolution, ask to speak with a supervisor or file an escalation via email at escalations@shopease.com.
