# Gift Cards

## Types of Gift Cards

### Digital Gift Cards
- Delivered instantly via email.
- Available in denominations: $10, $25, $50, $100, $200.
- Can be customized with a personal message and themed design.
- Recipient receives a unique gift card code.

### Physical Gift Cards
- Shipped in a premium gift box.
- Available in the same denominations as digital cards.
- Delivery takes 3–5 business days (free shipping).
- Available for purchase in-store at select retail partners.

## Using a Gift Card
1. Add items to your cart and proceed to checkout.
2. Enter your gift card code in the **Gift Card / Promo Code** field.
3. The balance will be applied to your order total.
4. If the order total exceeds the gift card balance, you can pay the remaining amount with another payment method.
5. If the order total is less than the gift card balance, the remaining balance stays on the card for future use.

## Checking Your Balance
- Go to **Account Settings > Gift Cards** and enter your card code.
- Or visit shopease.com/gift-card-balance.
- You can also call 1-800-SHOP-EZ and provide the card code.

## Terms and Conditions
- Gift cards do not expire.
- Gift cards cannot be returned or exchanged for cash (except where required by law).
- Lost or stolen gift cards cannot be replaced — treat them like cash.
- Gift cards can be used alongside other payment methods and promotional codes.
- Corporate bulk gift card purchases (50+) qualify for a 5% discount — contact sales@shopease.com.
