# Refund Process

## Refund Eligibility
You are eligible for a refund if:
- Your return request has been approved and the item has been received at our warehouse.
- You received a defective or damaged item.
- You received the wrong item.
- Your order was cancelled before shipping.

## Refund Methods

### Original Payment Method
- **Credit/Debit Card**: Refund appears in 5–10 business days.
- **PayPal**: Refund appears within 3–5 business days.
- **Apple Pay / Google Pay**: Refund appears within 5–7 business days.
- **Bank Transfer**: Refund appears within 7–14 business days.

### Store Credit
- Issued within 24 hours of return approval.
- Never expires.
- Can be used on any future purchase.
- Cannot be converted to cash.

## Partial Refunds
A partial refund may be issued if:
- The returned item shows signs of use.
- Accessories or parts are missing from the return.
- The item is returned outside the standard 30-day window (but within 60 days — subject to approval).

## Refund Status
Check your refund status anytime:
1. Go to **My Orders > Returns & Refunds**.
2. View the refund status for each return.
3. Statuses: Pending Review → Approved → Refund Initiated → Refund Completed.

## Disputes
If you believe your refund was incorrect, contact our support team within 15 days of receiving it. We'll review and correct any errors. For unresolved disputes, you can escalate via your bank or payment provider.
