# Return Policy

## Overview
At ShopEase, we want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase. If you're not happy with your order, you can return most items within **30 days** of delivery for a full refund or exchange.

## Eligibility
- Items must be in their original, unused condition with all tags attached.
- Items must be returned in their original packaging.
- Personalized or custom-made items cannot be returned.
- Perishable goods (food, flowers, etc.) are non-returnable.
- Undergarments and swimwear are final sale for hygiene reasons.

## How to Initiate a Return
1. Log in to your ShopEase account and go to **My Orders**.
2. Select the order containing the item you wish to return.
3. Click **Request Return** and choose a reason.
4. Print the prepaid return shipping label (free for most items).
5. Pack the item securely and drop it off at any authorized shipping location.

## Refund Timeline
- **Refund to original payment method**: 5–10 business days after we receive the item.
- **Store credit**: Issued within 24 hours of return approval.
- **Exchange**: New item shipped within 2–3 business days after receiving the return.

## Exceptions
- Electronics must be returned within 15 days and may be subject to a 15% restocking fee if opened.
- Large furniture items require a scheduled pickup — contact support to arrange.

## Contact
If you have questions about returns, email us at returns@shopease.com or call 1-800-SHOP-EZ.
