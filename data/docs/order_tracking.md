# Order Tracking

## How to Track Your Order
Once your order has shipped, you can track it in several ways:

### Via Your Account
1. Log in to your ShopEase account.
2. Go to **My Orders**.
3. Click on the order you want to track.
4. View the real-time tracking status and estimated delivery date.

### Via Email
We send tracking updates to your registered email at these stages:
- **Order Confirmed**: When your order is placed.
- **Order Shipped**: When it leaves our warehouse (includes tracking number).
- **Out for Delivery**: When it's on the delivery truck.
- **Delivered**: When it reaches your address.

### Via SMS
Opt in to SMS notifications in your account settings to receive text updates at each stage.

## Order Statuses Explained

| Status | What It Means |
|--------|--------------|
| Processing | Your order has been received and is being prepared |
| Confirmed | Payment verified, order is queued for packing |
| Packed | Items are packed and ready to ship |
| Shipped | Order has left our warehouse and is with the carrier |
| In Transit | Order is moving through the carrier's network |
| Out for Delivery | Package is on the delivery vehicle for your area |
| Delivered | Package has been delivered to the specified address |
| Cancelled | Order has been cancelled (refund initiated) |
| Returned | Return has been received at our warehouse |

## Multiple Shipments
Large orders may be split into multiple shipments. Each shipment gets its own tracking number. You'll see all tracking numbers under the order details in your account.
