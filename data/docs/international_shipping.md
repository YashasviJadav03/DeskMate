# International Shipping

## Countries We Ship To
ShopEase ships to over 50 countries worldwide, including Canada, United Kingdom, Australia, Germany, France, Japan, South Korea, India, Brazil, and Mexico. For a full list, check the dropdown at checkout.

## International Shipping Options

| Method | Delivery Time | Cost |
|--------|--------------|------|
| International Standard | 10–20 business days | $14.99 |
| International Express | 5–10 business days | $29.99 |
| International Priority | 3–5 business days | $49.99 |

## Customs & Duties
- International orders may be subject to customs duties, import taxes, and fees imposed by the destination country.
- **These charges are the responsibility of the recipient** and are not included in the shipping cost or product price.
- ShopEase is not responsible for delays caused by customs processing.
- We include a commercial invoice with all international shipments to facilitate customs clearance.

## Tracking International Orders
International orders are trackable at every stage. However, tracking details may be limited once the package reaches the destination country's postal system.

## Restrictions
- Some products cannot be shipped internationally due to local regulations (e.g., certain electronics, cosmetics, food items).
- Restricted items will be flagged at checkout.
- Maximum package weight for international shipping is 70 lbs (32 kg).

## Returns for International Orders
- International returns are accepted but return shipping costs are the customer's responsibility.
- We recommend using a trackable shipping method for returns.
- Refunds for international orders are processed within 10–15 business days after receiving the returned item.
- Customs duties paid on the original order are not refundable by ShopEase (contact your local customs office).
