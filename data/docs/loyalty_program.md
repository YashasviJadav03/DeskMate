# Loyalty Program — ShopEase Rewards

## How It Works
Earn points on every purchase and redeem them for discounts on future orders. Membership is free and automatic when you create a ShopEase account.

## Earning Points
- **1 point per $1 spent** on all purchases.
- **Double points** on ShopEase brand products.
- **Bonus points** during special promotions and sales events.
- **50 bonus points** for writing a product review.
- **100 bonus points** for referring a friend who makes a purchase.

## Membership Tiers

| Tier | Annual Spend | Benefits |
|------|-------------|----------|
| Bronze | $0–$299 | 1x points, birthday discount |
| Silver | $300–$999 | 1.5x points, free standard shipping, early sale access |
| Gold | $1,000–$2,499 | 2x points, free express shipping, exclusive deals |
| Platinum | $2,500+ | 3x points, free next-day shipping, personal shopper, VIP events |

## Redeeming Points

| Points | Reward |
|--------|--------|
| 500 | $5 off |
| 1,000 | $10 off |
| 2,500 | $25 off |
| 5,000 | $50 off + free gift |

Points can be applied at checkout. They cannot be combined with certain promotional codes. Points expire after 12 months of account inactivity.

## Referral Program
Share your unique referral link with friends. When they make their first purchase:
- **They get**: 15% off their first order
- **You get**: 100 bonus points

There's no limit to how many friends you can refer!
