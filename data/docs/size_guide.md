# Size Guide

## How to Measure
For the best fit, take your measurements while wearing light clothing:

### Chest / Bust
Measure around the fullest part of your chest, keeping the tape horizontal.

### Waist
Measure around your natural waistline, the narrowest part of your torso.

### Hips
Measure around the widest part of your hips and buttocks.

### Inseam
Measure from the top of your inner thigh to the bottom of your ankle.

## Tips
- If you're between sizes, we recommend sizing up for a more comfortable fit.
- Check individual product pages for model measurements and fit notes.
- Different brands may have slightly different sizing — check brand-specific size charts when available.
